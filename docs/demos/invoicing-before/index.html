How it works

    Your tax ID is enough: we fill in the company name and address automatically. You can upload your logo too.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Billcircle compliant with local regulations?

Yes. Invoices contain all the data required by VAT law, and reporting to the tax authority's online invoice system happens automatically.

I'm exempt from VAT as a small taxpayer. Can I use it?

Of course. You can set your VAT exemption and the required reference appears on your invoices automatically. We'll also warn you when you approach the VAT threshold.

Can I bring my customers over from another program?

Yes, you can import partners and products from a CSV or Excel file. If you get stuck, our support team helps with the switch for free.

What happens after the trial ends?

If you don't choose a paid plan, your account automatically switches to the Starter plan. Nothing is lost, and we never charge you unexpectedly.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored encrypted on servers within the European Union, in line with GDPR. Daily backups are made.
