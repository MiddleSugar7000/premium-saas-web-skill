Frequently asked questions

Before you get in touch

What size of company should use AI consulting?

We typically work with companies of 50 to 5,000 people, but more important than size is having repetitive, well-defined processes where automation brings noticeable savings.

How long until we see results?

The first working pilot is usually ready within 6-10 weeks. At the end of the discovery and strategy phase you already receive concrete proposals backed by an ROI calculation.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays under your control. We choose solutions that comply with GDPR and corporate security requirements and, where needed, run in an EU data center or on your own infrastructure.

Do we need our own IT or data team?

Not necessarily. We can take on delivery entirely, but if you have an internal team we work closely with them and hand over the knowledge needed to run the solution.

How do you price your work?

We do the discovery and strategy phase for a fixed fee, and delivery on a per-project basis or under a monthly retainer. The first consultation is free, and we follow up with a written proposal.
