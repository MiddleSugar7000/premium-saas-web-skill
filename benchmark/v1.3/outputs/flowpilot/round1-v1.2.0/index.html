How it works

Live in an afternoon. Trusted by Friday.

Connect your tools

Authorize Slack, Notion and Gmail with scoped, read-first permissions. Nothing is sent or edited until you say so.

SlackNotionGmail

  "gmail.readonly",
  "notion.pages:read",

02 / 03

Describe the job

Write what you want in a sentence. Flowpilot turns it into an agent with steps, triggers and a clear definition of done.

Done. Agent “Weekly Voice of Customer” created. Next run: Mon 8:00. Want a Notion copy too?
