Is it really a one-time $9?

Yes. One payment, no subscription, no upsell. Your license covers every update of this major version.

Does one license cover Mac and Windows?

One license works on both platforms, on up to three of your own computers.

Does it need an account or internet?

Neither. Clipnest never connects to the internet, so there is nothing to sign in to. Your license key is checked on your machine.

Where is my clipboard history stored?

In a local file on your computer. You can wipe it from the menu at any time, or set it to clear automatically.

What about passwords I copy?

Clipnest ignores copies from popular password managers by default, and you can add any app to the skip list.

Can I try it first?

If it is not for you, email within 30 days and you get your $9 back, no questions.

Does it sync between my devices?

No, and that is on purpose. Syncing would require a server, and a server means your clipboard leaves your computer.
