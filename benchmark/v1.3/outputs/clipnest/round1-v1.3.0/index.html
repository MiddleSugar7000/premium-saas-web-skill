FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version of how Clipnest works and what it costs.

Still have questions?hello@clipnest.example
Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. You buy a licence key, enter it once, and that's it.

Does Clipnest send my clipboard anywhere?

No. Your history is stored on your computer and Clipnest works fully offline. There's no cloud and nothing to sync.

Is $9 really one time?

Yes. One payment, no subscription. You won't be charged again for the version you buy.

Does one purchase cover Mac and Windows?

Clipnest is available for both. Check the checkout page for how many computers a licence covers.

Can I stop it from saving passwords?

You can choose apps to ignore, like your password manager, so copies from them are never saved. You can also delete any clip at any time.

Where is it in the menu bar?

On Mac it sits in the top menu bar. On Windows it lives in the system tray. Click it, or use your shortcut.
